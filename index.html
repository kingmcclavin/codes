TI-84 Plus CE

This page emulates the calculator's hardware: the eZ80 processor, flash and RAM, LCD, keypad and timers. The operating system comes from a ROM image of your own calculator.

A 4 MB TI-84 Plus CE dump (.rom). It stays in this browser and is never uploaded. You can also drop the file onto this page.
