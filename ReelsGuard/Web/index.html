    What the web version can and can't do

    • WorksPlays one Reel or post from a link, through Instagram's own embed player. There's no feed after it, and links inside the player can't open Instagram.
    • WorksDaily Reel time limit with a cooldown.
    • Can'tFilter instagram.com or the Instagram app. Instagram doesn't let other websites show its pages, so browsing your feed, profiles and messages has to happen in Instagram, or in the Reels Guard iPad app, which does filter them.
    • Can'tPlay Reels from private accounts, or Reels whose owner turned off embedding. Instagram shows a notice instead.
