Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

The short version
  • Your notebooks are stored on your own device. We don’t have accounts, servers holding your notes, ads, or analytics.
  • Basis is free and doesn’t sell or share your information. We never see your notes.
  • Two optional features use services you choose and sign in to yourself: AI (Claude or Gemini) and Cloud Sync (Google Drive). Your data goes straight from your device to that service.

Who we are

Basis is a free note-taking and calculator app that runs in your web browser. In this policy, “Basis”, “we” and “us” mean the people who make and run Basis. Questions? Contact us at CONTACT_EMAIL.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Basis (notebooks, handwriting, typed text, images, imported PDFs, folders, calculator history, formulas and settings) is saved in your browser’s storage on your device. It isn’t sent to us, and we can’t see it. Files you import, such as GoodNotes, Notability, PDF or .basis files, are opened and converted on your device.

Because your notes live in your browser, clearing your browser’s data for this site, or uninstalling Basis from your home screen, deletes them. Use Settings → Back Up Everything or Cloud Sync to keep a copy.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-ups with Basis.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or tracking cookies.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information.

Loading the app

Basis is hosted by Vercel. Like any website host, Vercel receives standard technical information when your browser loads Basis (such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested) and may keep it briefly to run and protect its service. Basis also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives the same kind of technical information when the fonts load. If Basis can’t load its own copy of its PDF tools, it loads them from cdnjs (Cloudflare). We don’t use any of this information to identify or track you.

Optional: AI (bring your own key)

If you choose to connect your own Claude (Anthropic) or Gemini (Google) account in the AI tab:

  • Your API key is stored only on your device. It isn’t included in backups and isn’t sent to us.
  • When Basis reads your handwriting (for search) or uses other AI features, it sends images or text of the pages involved directly from your device to the provider you chose, using your key. That provider handles your data under its own terms and privacy policy: Anthropic or Google. On Google’s free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products.
  • The text Basis gets back is saved with your notebook on your device.
  • Any cost is billed to your own account with that provider, not by Basis.

You can turn off automatic reading or disconnect at any time in the AI tab.

Optional: Cloud Sync (Google Drive)

If you choose to connect Google Drive in Settings → Cloud Sync:

  • Basis asks only for Google’s “drive.file” permission, which lets it see and change only the files it creates in your Drive. It can’t see your other files.
  • Basis creates a “Basis” folder in your Drive and keeps your notebooks, their images and PDFs, and your folder list there. Your notes go directly between your device and Google Drive.
  • To finish signing in, Google requires a small step on our server: your browser sends it a one-time sign-in code (or a sign-in renewal token), and our server passes Google’s reply back. It doesn’t store anything and never receives your notes.
  • Your Google sign-in tokens and email address are stored only on your device, so Basis can show which account is connected and keep syncing.

Basis’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google Drive access only to sync your Basis notebooks for you. We don’t use it for anything else, don’t transfer it to anyone, and don’t use it for advertising.

You can disconnect in Settings at any time, and remove Basis’s access from your Google Account permissions. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Children

Basis is for students and anyone else who takes notes. Because we don’t collect personal information, we don’t knowingly collect information from children. The optional AI and Google Drive features are subject to Anthropic’s and Google’s own age requirements.

Your choices

  • Delete notebooks in Basis, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings to remove everything on that device.
  • Disconnect AI or Cloud Sync whenever you like.
  • Export your notebooks at any time as PDF, PNG, .basis files or a full backup.

Security

Basis is served over HTTPS, and your notes and keys stay on your device unless you connect an optional service. Your device and browser’s own security (such as a passcode) protect the data stored on it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Basis handles data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. We’ll never start selling your data or showing ads.
